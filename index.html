From SketchUp to page

The 3D set is not the final art. It is the skeleton that keeps perspective right and saves hours on every panel.

1 Build it in SketchUp

Every wall, shelf and object modelled once.

2 Choose the shot

Move the camera like a film set: wide shots, close-ups, any lens.

3 Ink and color

Draw over the render by hand so it reads as comic art, not 3D.

About

I'm Taha Sabry, known as [LOOP], a self-taught Moroccan comic artist drawing since 2018. I write and draw my own stories and work with writers and publishers around the world. I build my backgrounds, vehicles and props in SketchUp, then finish every page by hand in Clip Studio Paint and Photoshop.
